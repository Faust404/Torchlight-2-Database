Torchlight II item cards

The tooltip card as settled — the P3 corner, the requirement block as chips, and the item's spawn band stated under it — on the eight items that between them carry every part of it.

The corner

Item level, sockets, and the class gate. The gate is a hard restriction on who may equip the item, so it lives here and is not repeated as a requirement.

Requirements

Each value boxed, with the or between the level and the attributes. A player needs one branch or the other, not both.

The spawn band

MINLEVEL to MAXLEVEL — the levels the item drops at. Plain text, because it is a fact about the item rather than a gate on the reader.

The locked group

An augmented weapon's bonus, stated as conditional: the task as a gold chip, the condition in words, and the stats it is holding behind a rule.

The one thing the block may never lose. A requirement is a player level or all of the attributes, whichever the player reaches first — not a conjunction. A reader who takes it for an "and" has been told something false about the item. The class gate in the corner is a separate, harder restriction on top of that.
The spawn band is not a screen. MINLEVEL and MAXLEVEL bracket the item's own level — Adeptus Helm is level 31 and drops between 28 and 34 — so they say where the item comes from, not who may use it. That is why they sit under the requirements in plain text rather than boxed like a third gate. The numbers print as the data has them: 999 says "no ceiling" on its own, and it is not the only one — 999999 and 9999999 both appear. Not every item has the field at all: 2,263 of the 5,334 weapons, armor and accessories carry at least one of the two, and it is the hand-authored items that have it — Legendary 100%, Set 95%, Unique 67%, Rare 31%, Normal 14%. A random magic item has no spawn band because its level comes from where it dropped.
Socketables keep their own line. A gem's MINLEVEL is read as a gate on the item it is socketed into — Required Item Level to Socket: 28 — and that phrase does not survive being shortened, so it is emitted whole. The spawn band is left off socketables entirely, so the same number never appears twice on one card under two different labels.
Blood Ember Shard has no affixes because the game gives it none. Its own .DAT is an empty shell, and the AFFIXES list on the _BASE file it inherits from is empty — it is list(2) on Flame, Ice, Spark and Venom Ember. Blood, Chaos, Iron and Void Ember are 32 gems that were cut before shipping: models, icons and names exist, effects were never written. The Flame Ember Shard beside it is the same rank of the same kind of gem, and is what a live one looks like.
