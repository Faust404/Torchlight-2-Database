Affix text: colour and font

Two questions about one block — the item's magic properties. Five colour treatments, then four settings of the face.

What the game does, and what it does not. The item rollover's own layout, MEDIA/UI/PIECES/EQUIPMENT_ROLLOVER.LAYOUT, colours text by what kind of block a line belongs to, never by what stat it grants: Effects and Sets headings take #1596EF, Enchantments #F7AF09, socket names #D6B300, socket effects #FBE204, flavour text #00FF40. The affix body itself carries no colour of its own — it is the one block the layout leaves to the game's magical green, #319C00.

So the game has no vocabulary for colouring one stat differently from the next, and this sheet does not invent one. Every variant below either reweights what is already in the string or restates the one colour the game does use.

Two candidates died before they got here. Element tint — sample the five element marks for a hue and paint an elemental affix line with it — fails on the art: the marks are drawn to be told apart by shape, and their most saturated pixels give physical #8C8131 against fire #89812F, two colours no one can separate, while poison lands on #3D9032, which is the magic green it would be replacing. Penalty marked by its sign fails on the data: 125 lines carry a negative number, and they are not penalties — -10% Physical Damage Taken and -1 to all item character stat requirements are both things the player wants.

Colour settled on C2

C2 is the one. It applies to the affix block the rule the card already uses two sections above it, where every damage value is set in --head against a --body label. C1 is what ships today; C3 to C5 are kept because the reasons for dropping them are the useful part.

Font the colour held at C1

F2 is the game's own tooltip setting — MEDIA/UI/ARIAL.FONT is media/ui/arial.TTF, size 14, Bold="true". The item name above the affixes is SerifBig in the rollover, which in this game is Sling Bold; it is copyright 1994 STAR Retrieval Systems, all rights reserved, so it is not in this repo and F4 tests the card's own serif instead.

C2, in each face the colour chosen, the face still open

The two axes are independent in the stylesheet, so these are the four cards as they would actually ship. The one thing to watch is the value's weight: C2 lifts it to 600 against 400 words, and F2 sets the whole line at 700 — so under Arial bold the words gain weight the value does not, and the emphasis rests on colour alone.
