Set bonuses: the ladder

One block, four questions — what it counts, the face it is set in, the values inside it, and the marker down its left edge — in the seven settings that turned out to be worth showing.

The heading quotes a number that is not a piece count. It reads 16 pieces (10 piece set) on Mondon's Vestment, and those are two different quantities wearing the same word. The 16 is a record count: sets.json's c is how many entries in items.json carry that setid, and Mondon's ships two item levels of the same seven armour slots — Mondon's at 99 and Outercore at 105 — plus one necklace and one ring, so 7×2+1+1=16 records.

The 10 is right, though, and the set does not have ten items — it has nine. The ladder counts pieces worn, and a character has two ring slots. That is the game's own slot list rather than an inference: MEDIA/INVENTORY/ ships HEAD TORSO SHOULDERS GLOVES PANTS BOOTS BELT NECKLACE RING1 RING2 RIGHTHAND LEFTHAND, and RING1.DAT and RING2.DAT differ only in their own names — both declare UNIT_TYPE = RING. Mondon's Ring fills both, so nine item types wear as ten pieces.

Which gives the number the dimming should be using. A set's capacity is 2 for a ring, 2 for each one-handed weapon type, 1 for everything else — and 52 of the 80 ladders top out at exactly that, with none above it but Cornerstone, whose seven single-slot items carry a nine-rung ladder. Xtro's Blades is the clearest case: a claw and a ring, two item types, a ladder that runs to 4. Twinferno is the same shape and names itself for it — one wand, a ladder of two, worn as a pair.

The shipped test is rung > c, the record count, and it errs in one direction only: it dims 15 rungs across 14 sets that a character can reach, and admits none that it cannot. Twinferno's only rung is one of the 15 — the card dims the single bonus of a set built to be dual-wielded.

The card's heading keeps both numbers and gives each its own word — 16 items · 10 piece set — so neither is lost and the pair no longer contradicts itself. That is S8 below, and it is what the card ships.

Variations S1 is what ships today · S8 is what the card now carries

Three specimens. Mondon's Belt is the inflated heading — 16 records, 9 item types, 10 worn — and the only ladder long enough to read as a ladder. Twinferno Wand is one wand with a two-piece ladder, which is the 1 pieces bug and the wrongly dimmed rung at once. Aristocrat Belt is a set that agrees with itself on every count, and the only specimen with a rung carrying four bonuses, which is where the blank continuation marker appears.
